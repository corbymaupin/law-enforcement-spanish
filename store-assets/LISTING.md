## Full description
Law Enforcement Spanish helps English-speaking police officers, county jail officers, and LE personnel learn the Spanish they need for intake, booking, and in-facility interactions with Spanish-speaking detainees.

295 cards across 9 categories: Intake, Medical, Rights, Commands, Facility, De-escalation, Questions, Paperwork, and Phrases.

How it works:
• Study mode with Leitner spaced repetition — cards you miss come back sooner
• Listen mode for hands-free audio practice using your phone’s offline text-to-speech
• Browse the full deck by category
• Track streak and progress on the Stats screen
• In-app Feedback to report bugs or ideas

Built for the field and the facility:
• Works fully offline after install — no account, no ads, no network required while studying
• Progress stays on your device only
• Practical phrases for Miranda/rights, medical intake, commands, and de-escalation — not classroom fluff

Made for officers and jail staff who need clear, usable Spanish on shift.

One-time purchase ($1.99). No ads, no subscription, no account.
